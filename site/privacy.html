Your accounts, passwords and manuscript records stay in your own browser. The extension only connects to the submission sites you use. We have no server and can’t see any of it.

如何保存How it is stored

  • 所有数据都存在浏览器给这个扩展的专用存储里，不走浏览器同步，也不上传到任何服务器。
  • All data is kept in the browser storage that belongs to this extension. It is not synced through your browser account and not uploaded to any server.
  • 密码用 AES-GCM 加密后保存。密钥由浏览器在安装时生成，无法导出，只存在你的电脑上。
  • Passwords are encrypted with AES-GCM. The key is generated by the browser at install time, is marked non-exportable and exists only on your computer.

扩展如何联网How the extension uses the network

  • 只访问查询稿件状态所需的页面：你使用的投稿系统、Elsevier 追踪接口，以及你添加并授权的网站。移除网站时权限一并收回。
  • It only opens the pages needed to read manuscript status: the submission systems you use, Elsevier’s tracking service, and sites you add and authorize yourself. Access is revoked when you remove a site.
  • IEEE 等系统只能在网页中登录，扩展会在最小化窗口中登录，完成后关闭。
  • For systems such as IEEE that require a real page to sign in, the extension opens a minimized browser window and closes it when the check is done.
  • 只读：不会提交、修改或撤回任何稿件。密码被平台拒绝一次后就不再自动重试，以免账号被锁。
  • Read-only: it never submits, edits or withdraws a manuscript. After a platform rejects a password once, it stops retrying so your account is not locked.
  • 扩展不连接本网站，也不连接开发者的任何服务器。扩展里没有统计、广告或追踪代码，也不加载远程代码。
  • The extension does not contact this website or any developer server. It contains no analytics, ads or tracking code, and loads no remote code.

我们不做的事What we don’t do

  • 不收集、不出售、不转让你的数据，也不与任何第三方共享。
  • We do not collect, sell or transfer your data, and we do not share it with any third party.
  • 不读取你的浏览历史，不在与投稿无关的网页上运行。
  • We do not read your browsing history or run on pages unrelated to your submissions.
  • 不把数据用于广告、信用评估或与本扩展功能无关的任何用途。
  • We do not use any data for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose unrelated to the extension’s single function.

诊断信息Diagnostics

只有你在设置里点「复制诊断信息」时，扩展才会把版本号、浏览器版本、设置、各平台的稿件数量和最近的日志复制到剪贴板，不含账号、密码和稿件题目。是否把它贴到 GitHub Issues 由你决定。

Only when you click “Copy diagnostics” in Settings does the extension copy its version, browser version, settings, manuscript counts per platform and recent log lines to your clipboard. It contains no usernames, passwords or manuscript titles. You decide whether to paste it into GitHub Issues.

删除数据Deleting your data

在「设置」里点「全部清除」，会删除所有账号、密钥、稿件记录和日志。卸载扩展时，浏览器也会一并删除这些数据。

Click “Clear all” in Settings to delete every account, key, manuscript record and log entry. Uninstalling the extension also deletes all of this data.

本网站This website

tracker.eonmaze.com 是静态网页，不设置 Cookie。我们用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 统计访问量，它不使用 Cookie、不识别个人，只汇总浏览次数、来源网站、地区和浏览器类型。服务器和 Cloudflare 会产生常规访问日志（含 IP 地址、时间和访问的网址），仅用于网站运行和安全防护。

tracker.eonmaze.com is a static website and sets no cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, doesn’t identify individuals, and only aggregates page views, referring sites, region and browser type. The server and Cloudflare keep standard access logs (IP address, time and URL), used only to run and protect the site.
