连接投稿账号，开始自动追踪

Connect your submission accounts to start tracking

选择投稿的期刊类型并填写账号，扩展将定时查询每篇稿件的状态，并在状态变化时通知你。

Choose where you submit and add the account. The extension checks every manuscript on a schedule and notifies you when a status changes.

也可以直接在投稿网站正常登录一次，扩展会自动保存该账号。

You can also sign in on the submission website as usual; the extension saves the account automatically.

每类期刊只需连接一次。账号使用本机生成的密钥加密，仅保存在本浏览器中，只会发送到对应的投稿网站。也可以不在此填写，直接在投稿网站登录一次，扩展会自动保存。

Connect each type of journal once. Accounts are encrypted with a key generated on this device, stored only in this browser and sent only to the matching submission website. You can also skip this and sign in on the submission website once; the extension saves the account automatically.

状态变化时通知Notify on status changes发送桌面通知。首次查询只记录当前状态，不发通知。Shows a desktop notification. The first check only records the current status.
